Research

Research Focus

GPU memory systems

I design memory-management mechanisms for GPU servers under memory pressure, including prefetching, placement, migration, fault recovery, and device-side reclamation.

SAFE · ARIADNE · ReclaimX

Heterogeneous CPU–GPU execution

I treat the CPU as an active compute resource in GPU-centric workloads, reducing host-memory traffic and restructuring work so CPU execution overlaps with GPU computation instead of extending the critical path.

ReFlow · ReplayOpt

Workload-aware AI scaling

I exploit workload-specific sparsity and locality, including expert routing in Mixture-of-Experts and view-local working sets in 3D Gaussian Splatting, to scale training beyond conventional GPU-memory limits.

Current MoE research · Current 3DGS research

Architecture to full systems

I work at the abstraction level that best matches the bottleneck—from GPU architecture and full-system simulation to driver/runtime mechanisms, SIMD/CUDA optimization, and end-to-end training systems.
