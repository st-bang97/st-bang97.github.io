RESEARCH ACTIVITIES

ReFlow / ReplayOpt: CPU-Offloaded LLM Training
ASPLOS 2027 / IEEE CAL 2026
  • Conventional offloading approaches primarily hide host-side update latency; profiling instead identified unnecessary host-memory traffic and repeated gradient movement as the dominant costs, rather than CPU optimizer arithmetic.
  • Redesigned DeepSpeed ZeRO-3 around CPU-side gradient processing, register-resident SIMD fusion, and dependency-aware Ready/Persist scheduling, separating GPU-critical parameter generation from optimizer-state work that can finish later.
  • Validated training semantics and regression behavior across Adam/AdamW and Lion, clipping, overflow, checkpointing, and single-/multi-GPU systems including NVIDIA B200, A100, and RTX PRO GPUs; achieved up to 4.0× throughput over ZeRO-Infinity in evaluated configurations.
Code: https://github.com/st-bang97/DeepSpeed/tree/PR_branch
Example: https://github.com/st-bang97/DeepSpeedExamples/tree/PR_branch
ReclaimX: Device-Side Reclamation under UVM Oversubscription
MICRO 2026
  • Rather than accepting the host-driven UVM reclamation path while GPU resources wait on page faults, reinterpreted fault-stalled GPU execution capacity as an opportunity to perform reclamation.
  • Designed fine-grained victim ordering, selective compressed residency, and GPU-local restoration to reduce host-mediated recovery; evaluated the architecture through cycle-level GPU/UVM simulation.
Code: https://github.com/st-bang97/ReclaimX_MICRO2026
