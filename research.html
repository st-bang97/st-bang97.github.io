Research & Publications

Efficient GPU Servers, from Architecture to Full Systems

Vision

Making GPU servers more efficient under memory and data-movement pressure

My research asks how GPU servers can sustain larger and more demanding AI workloads when memory capacity, host-device movement, and serialized execution become the bottleneck. I approach this problem across the system stack—from GPU architecture and memory-system simulation to driver/runtime mechanisms and full-system training implementations—so that architectural ideas can be carried through to end-to-end server behavior.

Research

Memory systems for efficient GPU servers

I redesign GPU memory management under oversubscription, covering prefetching, memory placement, far-page-fault recovery, migration, and device-side reclamation. The goal is to reduce host-mediated overheads while making more of the GPU's effective memory capacity usable.

SAFE · ARIADNE · ReclaimX

Heterogeneous CPU–GPU execution

I study how CPU resources can participate actively in GPU-centric workloads rather than serving only as slow overflow storage. My work on CPU-offloaded LLM training reduces host-memory traffic, restructures optimizer execution, and overlaps CPU and GPU work to remove host-side critical-path bottlenecks.

ReFlow · ReplayOpt

Workload-aware AI scaling

I exploit workload-specific sparsity and locality to scale training beyond conventional GPU-memory limits. Current work uses routing behavior in Mixture-of-Experts models and view-local working sets in 3D Gaussian Splatting to determine what must reside on the GPU and when CPU-side state can be processed or transferred.

Current MoE research · Current 3DGS research

Architecture to full systems

I work across multiple abstraction levels: GPU architecture and memory-system studies with Accel-Sim, full-system modeling with gem5, NVIDIA GPU-driver and UVM mechanisms, SIMD-optimized CPU execution, CUDA/PyTorch training systems, and end-to-end server evaluation. This lets me evaluate a mechanism not only as an isolated architectural idea, but also as part of a complete hardware–software system.
