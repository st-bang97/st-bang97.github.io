Areas

GPU memory systems

My research on GPU memory systems covers UVM oversubscription, far-page-fault recovery, prefetching, migration, and memory management, with the goal of reducing host-mediated overheads while exposing more usable GPU memory capacity.

SAFE · ARIADNE · ReclaimX

LLM and MoE training systems

My research investigates scalable training for large language and Mixture-of-Experts models on heterogeneous CPU–GPU systems, with an emphasis on optimizer execution, memory efficiency, data movement, and execution overlap.

ReplayOpt · Current MoE research

Scalable 3D Gaussian Splatting

My research includes systems techniques for scaling 3D Gaussian Splatting training when scene growth places sustained pressure on GPU memory and data movement.

Current 3DGS research

Architecture and simulation

My architecture research focuses on GPU architecture and device-side memory-management mechanisms, using Accel-Sim, gem5, and full-system simulation to evaluate GPU, memory, and networking systems.

ReclaimX · pNet-gem5
