Research

Systems for memory-constrained AI.

My work focuses on the interaction among GPU memory management, heterogeneous CPU–GPU execution, and the training systems that depend on them.

GPU Memory Systems

I study UVM oversubscription, far-page-fault recovery, prefetching, migration, and reclamation. The goal is to reduce host-mediated overheads and expose more memory capacity without sacrificing GPU throughput.

Efficient LLM Training

I investigate CPU-offloaded training, optimizer scheduling, gradient lifetime, and overlap across CPU and GPU execution. The emphasis is on moving memory-intensive training state off the critical path while retaining scalable GPU execution.

Architecture & Simulation

I use and extend architectural simulators and full-system infrastructure to evaluate GPU, memory, and networking mechanisms with controlled microarchitectural detail.
